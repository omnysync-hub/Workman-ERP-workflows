Section 1

The Big Picture: How a Job Passes Hand to Hand

Every single job moves through 5 teams in the exact same order. The computer system automatically sends alerts at each step, so nobody has to waste time calling coworkers to ask "is this done yet?"

Team 1: Dispatcher
Takes the Call

Finds the customer, lists what needs fixing with prices, and picks the best technician for the job .

Team 2: Technician
Does the Work

Gets the alert on their phone, arrives on site, uses parts, does the labor, and marks it finished .

Team 3: Accountant
Daily Hisaab (Settlement)

Takes the cash collected from the customer, checks worker expenses, and confirms leftover parts were returned .

Team 4: Admin / Boss
Final Check & Approval

Reviews the work, confirms the cash and stock match up, and signs off . The job is now locked .

Team 5: Call Center
Calls the Customer

Calls the customer to ask if they are happy with the work and marks down their review .

Section 3

What Each Person Taps and Does (Step by Step)

Click any role below to expand their exact daily checklist. This explains what buttons they see and what happens in the background .

Dispatcher
Taking Calls, Opening Jobs & Scheduling
▾
  1. Customer Calls In: Dispatcher searches by customer phone number or name .
  2. Autofill or New Customer: If they exist, details load instantly . If new, adds them and selects their location from an interactive map .
  3. Job Ticket Created: System automatically creates a unique job tracking number .
  4. List the Scope: Dispatcher selects what needs to be done (e.g., "AC Gas Refill", "Wiring Repair") and enters standard prices and estimated quantities .
  5. Subcontractor Check: If this job is subcontracted from another partner company, attaches that company's name and billing info .
  6. Assign Technician: Picks an available worker from a list or looks at a live GPS map showing who is nearest to the customer's house .
  7. Automatic Alert: The moment they hit save, the technician receives both a WhatsApp message and a mobile app notification .
Technician
Mobile App on Site, Live Discounts, Material Requests & Work
▾
  1. View New Job: Opens the phone notification to see customer name, address, phone, and what work is required .
  2. Accept & Start: Taps Accept . When arriving at the customer's doorstep, taps Start Job (this records arrival time and GPS location) .
  3. Need More Parts Mid-Job? Taps Request Parts in the app . The store keeper instantly receives the request on their screen .
  4. Request Live Discounts on Parts & Labor: If a customer negotiates on site for a lower price on parts (like a capacitor, copper pipe) or labor services, the technician taps Request Discount directly in the app . They choose the item, type the requested discounted rate, and send it to the office . The worker cannot apply discounts on their own—it must be approved by the accountant or boss . As soon as the office clicks approve, the new lower price updates on the technician's phone screen instantly .
  5. Something Damaged or Lost? If an item gets broken or lost on site, reports it in the app . This notifies both the store and accountant .
  6. Work Stops for the Night: If the job will take another day, taps Stop Job . The app warns them: they must hand back unused parts to the store and bring the day's cash to the accountant before tomorrow's resume button will work .
  7. Out-of-Pocket Spending: If the technician buys tape or screws from a local market, they enter the amount, write a note, and take a photo of the receipt .
  8. Finishing the Job: Taps Complete Job . The app requires them to enter the exact actual quantity of parts and labor used before allowing them to submit .
Accountant
Daily Cash Settling (Hisaab), Live Discount Approvals & Freezing Records
▾
  1. Daily Queue: Opens their screen to see all jobs waiting for settlement (both paused multi-day jobs and completed jobs) .
  2. Live Discount Approvals: Receives instant pop-ups when technicians on site request a discount for parts or labor . Can approve or reject with one click, automatically updating the customer's live job bill .
  3. Calculated Total: The system displays the exact amount owed by the customer based on actual parts and labor used, minus any approved discounts .
  4. Enter Cash Handed Over: Enters the actual paper cash given by the technician . If the customer paid less than full, the system saves the unpaid shortfall and sets a reminder date to collect the rest .
  5. Audit Worker Expenses: Reviews the photo receipts uploaded by the technician . If office cash is available, reimburses them on the spot . If cash is low, pays a portion or defers it . Any unpaid amount stays permanently recorded on the technician's balance so they never lose money .
  6. Check Store Sign-Off: Confirms that the store keeper physically received leftover parts . If items were lost or broken by the technician, the cost is charged to the technician's personal ledger .
  7. Lock the Record: Clicks Finalize . The numbers are now frozen—nobody can change prices or items anymore—and the job goes to the boss for final audit .
Admin / Supervisor
Final Approval, Live GPS Map & High-Level Reports
▾
  1. Approval Inbox: Sees every finalized job ready for sign-off in one simple list .
  2. Full History: Can see live worker locations, how many hours the job took, and every action taken from start to finish .
  3. Quick 3-Point Checklist: Ticks 3 boxes: Was the work actually done? Was the money collected? Were excess parts returned? If anything is suspicious, sends it back with a note—nothing is silently changed .
  4. Final Verification: Hits Approve . The job moves to "Verified" status and automatically goes to the Call Center .
  5. Live Team Map: Looks at a map showing where all technicians are in the city to assign urgent jobs efficiently .
  6. Executive Dashboards: Views one clean screen showing completed jobs, pending money, total revenue, and worker rankings .
Store Keeper
Handing Out Parts, Receiving Returns & Stock Levels
▾
  1. Issues Materials: Sees parts requested by technicians on their phone app, collects them from warehouse shelves, and marks them as handed out .
  2. Receives Leftovers: Physically checks returned parts from technicians at the end of the day and clicks "Received" . (This unlocks the technician's phone for the next day) .
  3. Handles Missing/Broken Items: If a technician broke or lost a part, confirms the item is gone so the accountant can deduct the cost from the technician .
  4. Maintains the Catalog: Adds new items, sets minimum safety stock alerts, and updates standard prices .
  5. Reorders Stock: When items get low on shelves, clicks to create a purchase request for management to approve .
HR & Payroll
Biometric Attendance, Salary Advances & Monthly Pay
▾
  1. Check-In / Check-Out: Workers scan their face and app verifies their GPS location twice a day . Any failed attempt is saved so nobody can fake attendance .
  2. Salary Advances: When a worker takes an advance loan, it is recorded against their personal balance .
  3. 3-Step Pay Day (Safety Rule):
    • Step 1: System automatically calculates salaries, subtracting advance loans and lost items. (Draft only, hidden from workers) .
    • Step 2: A different manager must review and click "Approve" (so one person cannot send money out alone) .
    • Step 3: Salaries are marked paid, company accounts update, and pay slips appear on worker phones .
Call Center
Calling Customers to Confirm Satisfaction
▾
  1. Automated Queue: Every time the boss approves a job, it pops up directly on the Call Center caller screen .
  2. Customer Feedback Call: Agent dials the customer and chooses one of four buttons: Happy, Unhappy, No Answer, or Call Later .
  3. Unhappy Customer? If the customer is dissatisfied, the agent must type the reason . This triggers an urgent red flag directly on the Admin's screen . (Note: money already collected is not touched) .
  4. No Answer? System sets a timer to bring the customer's phone number back to the screen later in the afternoon .
  5. Ratings Dashboard: Feedback scores calculate the technician's monthly quality rating .
Sales & Accounts
Quotations, Invoices & Shop Counter Sales
▾
  1. Quotation to Job: Can build a price estimate for a customer . When the customer agrees, one click turns it into an active job ticket .
  2. Automatic Customer Invoices: When a technician completes a job, the system turns the real quantities into an official printable invoice .
  3. Counter Sales (Shop POS): If a walk-in customer buys a filter or thermostat at your office counter, the cashier selects the item, prints an invoice, and the inventory drops immediately .

Section 6

What Happens When Items Are Broken or Lost

Instead of items disappearing with nobody taking responsibility, the system has a clean 3-step path that holds the right person accountable .

Step 1: Technician
Reports the Loss

Reports in the mobile app: "Item X was broken / missing on site."

Step 2: Store Keeper
Confirms Item is Missing

Checks physical stock, verifies the part is gone, and updates the warehouse count .

Step 3: Accountant
Charges Worker Ledger

System charges the cost of the lost item directly against the technician's salary balance .

Why this matters: Everything is recorded openly . There are no side arguments at the end of the month because the proof is saved on the system .

Section 7

Reimbursing Worker Expenses (Cash Flow Rule)

Technicians often spend their own money to buy small parts like tape, gas, or pipe on site . Here is how the accountant pays them back based on how much cash the office has .

Technician uploads receipt photo → Accountant reviews at end of day:
Office Has Full Cash: Reimbursed 100% on the spot Office Has Some Cash: Paid partially, remainder carried forward Office Has No Cash: Full amount saved as company debt to worker

The Golden Rule: If the office cannot pay today, the money is never forgotten . It stays on the technician's balance sheet until cash arrives, or it is automatically added as a bonus on their next monthly paycheck .

Section 8

Your Price List: Physical Parts vs Labor Services

Your system handles physical products and labor charges separately so your warehouse counts stay accurate .

Physical Parts (Managed by Store)
Items with Stock Count

Copper pipes, gas cans, thermostats, motors . When used on a job, the warehouse count goes down .

Labor Services (Managed by Boss)
Intangible Work (No Stock)

AC Service Fee, Gas Refill Labor, Installation Charge . Just a name and a fixed selling price .

Everywhere Else
Both Pickable Together

Both appear neatly in a dropdown menu whenever staff make an estimate, bill, or work order .

Section 9

Buying New Stock (Stopping Theft & Overpaying)

How your business buys materials from suppliers using a strict 5-step check so you never pay for items that did not arrive .

1. Store Keeper
Requests Stock

Notices parts are low or system warns that shelves hit minimum limit .

2. Boss
Approves Request

Checks if company has budget and approves purchase .

3. Purchasing
Sends PO to Supplier

Generates official PDF Purchase Order with locked prices and emails vendor .

4. Store Keeper
Inspects Delivery

When delivery truck arrives, counts items physically and records what actually came in .

5. Accountant
Checks & Pays Bill

Performs the 3-Way Match before paying the bill .

The 3-Way Match Rule: The accountant will only pay the supplier bill if:
What was Ordered (PO) == What was Received (Store Slip) == What the Supplier Billed (Invoice) .
If the supplier charges for 10 items but only 8 arrived at your warehouse, the system blocks full payment automatically .
